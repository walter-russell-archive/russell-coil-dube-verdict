LEE ENGINEERING COMPANY
TELEPHONE WEST 3-2100
2023 WEST WISCONSIN AVENUE
MILWAUKEE 3 · WISCONSIN

Mr. Walter Russell
Swannanoa
Waynesboro, Virginia

Dear Mr. Russell:

Thank you for your letter of January 2nd requesting a copy
of the Tesla book for Mr. Maurice Miller which we are happy to
send.  I am sorry I had not previously acknowledged your open
letter to the world of science under the date of February 3, 1953.
It is a tremendous presentation and I certainly appreciate your
difficulty in getting scientific minds interested in the ideas
communicated therein.  We really live in a world of slanted pub-
licity in which there seems to be a politically inspired repres-
sion of many facts.  We possibly run into more of that than any-
one because of our contact with nutritional foods in which pro-
moters seem to be quite successful in keeping the public in the
dark about the effects of those foods.  I enclose a copy of a
lecture that will give you a few ideas on the subject.  Heart
disease would disappear if we would not have to eat refined foods.

Very truly yours,

LEE ENGINEERING COMPANY

## Document 2 — Walter Russell to Dr. Royal Lee (journal p. 16 / PDF 19)

March 20th 1954.

Dear Dr. Lee:

I cannot tell you how pleased my wife (Lao) and I were to
receive a copy of the Tesla book which you reprinted for the benefit
of thinkers in science. Nicola Tesla and I exchanged inspirations for
many years. He was an artist at heart whom the world knew as a scientist-
while I was a scientist at heart whom the world knew as an artist.

He was my spiritual mainstay while I produced my greatest
work in sculpture - The Mark Twain Memorial, and I likewise spiritually
supported when his soul was low from doubtings and attacks by lesser
minds.

I give you these reasons why I especially thank you for the
book-- but I also add an appreciation for your vision which indicates a
love and admiration for him such as had grown in me.

I could never understand why Nicola Tesla was not proclaimed
the greatest man in science of his day - for he was just that and your
gesture in printing that book makes me feel deeply indebted to you in
my friend's name.

Thinking you may be interested to know of our work here -
and my unrewarded contributions to science which have received even
less recognition than Nicola's - having given the six transuranium
elements to the world as you will see by the enclosed charts - I enclose
a little booklet of my life and my second open letter to science for
your perusal.

Mrs. Russell joins me in extending to you a cordial invitation
to visit our mountain top shrine at any time you may feel like looking at
the other side of the medal upon which is written the constitution of
matter from Nature's point of view to replace the unnatural one written
on the visible side by man.

Thanking you again and looking forward to a interchange of
thoughts whenever you feel so inclined, we extend to you our good wishes
and greetings.

Sincerely yours

Walter Russell.

P. S. You ask if I might wish to send one to any other Tesla admirer.
[Dr?] John O'Neill, Science editor of the Herald Tribune, wrote an excellent
book of Tesla's life, entitled PRODIGAL GENIUS of which I have an
autographed copy.  I am sure he would be delighted to exchange copies
with you, and I am sure you would like his book.

## Notes

- **Chronology:** the printed order is reversed. Russell's letter (Mar 20, 1954) thanks Lee for the reprinted Tesla book. Lee's letter (Jan 6, 1955) answers a *later* Russell letter of Jan 2 [1955], which requested a further copy of the Tesla book for a Mr. Maurice Miller. The "Tesla book" Lee reprinted is very likely John J. O'Neill's *Prodigal Genius* (mentioned in Russell's P.S.) or a Tesla lecture reprint. The letters do not name the reprint's title.
- **Bearing on the "Tesla told Russell to bury his knowledge for 1,000 years" folklore:** these letters neither state nor allude to any such instruction. Russell himself characterizes the relationship as mutual inspiration and emotional support ("Nicola Tesla and I exchanged inspirations for many years", "He was my spiritual mainstay… I likewise spiritually supported when his soul was low"). No mention of burying, sealing, or withholding knowledge appears anywhere. Moreover, the 1996 USP editor states flatly that this letter "is the only item in the archives that shows any trace of Walter Russell and Nikola Tesla's association". He also appeals to readers for any actual correspondence. That is, as of 1996 the USP archive held **no documentary support** for the folklore quote. The letters therefore *undercut* the legend's documentary basis. They prove acquaintance, but the famous "bury it" line rests on no archived Tesla document. Notably, in the coil correspondence three years later (journal p. 21), Russell argues the *opposite* of secrecy: "the great secrecy which we thought necessary was not at all necessar[y]".
- Russell's claim of "having given the six transuranium elements to the world" refers to his 1926 periodic-chart predictions. His spelling "Nicola" (for Nikola) is as typed.
- The illegible handwritten note on Lee's letter appears to list enclosures (a malnutrition pamphlet and a reprint list), consistent with "I enclose a copy of a lecture".
