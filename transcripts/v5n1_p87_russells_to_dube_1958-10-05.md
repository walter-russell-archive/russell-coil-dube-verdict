Dear Mr. Dube;

Your letter of the 3rd inst. lifted us up into the high
heavens of happiness. Your revision, made necessary by
small scale, is perfect. We did not want the hole for this
test anyhow but will need it when we begin to tear the
elements apart with fifty to a hundred pound coils to
transmute them.

We will then make revisions of the design which will make
the central solenoids smaller and grade them up in size
because less turns on large diameters will require less
space. This will keep the outside sphere shape _as is_
and would help make the inner cathode hole more spherical.
It is not worth while doing now while your tools are set
as they are, but it would be worth while doing when you
have made this test and find it works, just to find out
in small scale how much extra percentage of lifting power
can be added to the poles.

My _intuitive_ mathematics are often very near being right.
Applying that method to the design now under way I venture
to predict a 40% to 60% increase in gravity power of the
Russell coils over the pair of conventional ones, as you
are now making them. By changing the middle solenoid
diameters for refinement, as above suggested, it should
add between 5% and 10%. It will be interesting to see how
near our prophesy [sic] comes true.

Again expressing our happiness for having been able to
have you so clearly understand the underlying principles
which must be comformed [sic] to in order to mechanically
interpret Nature's process of power multiplication, and
thanking you for your patient follow through with us,
we are,

          Sincerely yours

          [signature: Walter & Lao Russell]

          Walter and Lao Russell.

## Notes

- This letter replies to a Dube letter "of the 3rd inst." (October 3, 1958), which this issue does not reprint. That Dube letter evidently transmitted the revised test-unit drawings LO 8308-11 / LO 8308-12 dated Oct 2, 1958 (journal pp. 93–94). Those drawings delete the through-hole that Russell requested Aug 30.
- This puts Russell's quantitative prediction on record before the test: the Russell coils were to out-pull the conventional pair by 40–60%. The measured result (Fig. 4, journal p. 92, dated Nov 1958) shows that the step-diameter (Russell) coils pull *less* than the standard coils at every gap. This is the "Dube verdict."
- "comformed" and "prophesy" are preserved as in the facsimile typescript. In "patient follow through with us", the typescript drops the final h of "through" to subscript and runs it into "with" with no space ("througₕwith" — a slipped key). The transcript normalizes the spacing.
