# John E. Dube (Alco Valve Company) to Walter Russell — Cover Letter for Coil Test Report

- **Source issue:** The Fulcrum, Vol. 4, No. 3 (1996), "From The Archives" section
- **Journal page:** 17
- **PDF page:** 20 (fulcrum_v4n3_october_1996.pdf)
- **Date:** November 21, 1958
- **From:** John E. Dube, President, Alco Valve Company, St. Louis, Mo.
- **To:** Dr. Walter Russell, University of Science and Philosophy, Swannanoa, Waynesboro, Virginia
- **CC:** Mr. Russell Maguire
- **Conventions:** the transcription is verbatim. `[illegible]` = unreadable. `[word?]` = uncertain reading.

---

ALCO VALVE COMPANY

ST. LOUIS, MO.

November 21, 1958

JOHN E. DUBE
PRESIDENT

Dr. Walter Russell,
University of Science and Philosophy,
Swannanoa, Waynesboro, Virginia

Dear Dr. Russell:

Attached find report of comparison tests between a Russell
and an orthodox solenoid coil.  You will note that we were
unable to detect any advantage for the Russell coil.

Under separate cover we are sending you the two pairs of
test coils to give you a personal opportunity to check our
results.

Please let us know if you want further information concern-
ing this matter.

Yours very truly,

[signature: John E. Dube]
John E. Dube

JED:ER
Encl.

CC: Mr. Russell Maguire

---

## Notes

- This is the cover letter for the "Report on Evaluation of Russell Coil" printed on journal pp. 18–19 (see v4n3_p18_evaluation_report.md).
- One sentence delivers the verdict: "we were unable to detect any advantage for the Russell coil." Dube also returned the physical test coils to Russell so that Russell could replicate the measurements himself. This was a notably fair-minded gesture.
- The CC to Russell Maguire (Greenwich, Conn.) identifies the financier/backer of the test program. Russell's Nov. 24, 1958 reply (journal pp. 20–22) is addressed to Maguire with a copy to Dube. This confirms Maguire as the sponsor who was "withdrawing" after the negative result.
